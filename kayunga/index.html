Kayunga

The place she represents.

Every priority she has championed traces back to this district — where she was born, taught, organized, and eventually was elected to represent.

Harriet Nakwedde was born in Busaana Sub-county, Kayunga District. Long before she held office, she was a visible presence in local political life there — building relationships across the district's sub-counties over two decades of campaigning, organizing, and, eventually, governing.

Her priorities are drawn directly from that time on the ground: keeping girls in school as a former teacher; pressing for infrastructure commitments to be honored, including the 2023 protest over an unfulfilled pledge to tarmac a district road; and founding the Forum for Emancipation to Empowerment Drive (FEED) to extend that work into women's economic independence and grassroots civic participation across the district.
